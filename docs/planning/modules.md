# Project Modules

This document provides an overview of the major modules included in the Tassawaq E-Commerce platform.

---

## Authentication
Responsible for user authentication, authorization, and account security.

---

## Users
Manages user profiles, addresses, and account information.

---

## Products
Manages the product catalog, including product information, inventory, pricing, images, and availability.

---

## Categories
Organizes products into categories.

---

## Brands
Manages product brands.

---

## Reviews
Allows customers to rate and review products.

---

## Cart
Handles shopping cart operations.

---

## Wishlist
Allows customers to save products for later.

---

## Orders
Manages order creation, tracking, and order history.

---

## Coupons
Handles discount coupons and promotions.

---

## Payments
Handles payment processing, payment status, and transaction records.

---

## Notifications
Handles email and system notifications.

---

## Admin Dashboard
Provides administrative capabilities for managing the platform, including users, products, orders, categories, brands, coupons, and system settings.
